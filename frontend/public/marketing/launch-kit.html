04

What the website costs you

Ads send people to kioscart.com, so the page has to finish the argument. Ten things I found reading it as a shopkeeper would, worst first.

  • 01

    Four of your six modules are invisible

    Accounting export, feedback and replies, the AI chat, and the payment methods themselves never appear. "Payments — PCI-compliant secure processing" hides your strongest hook behind a compliance word. Name them: QR, Cards, Net banking.

    Fix first
  • 02

    No product screenshots anywhere

    Nobody buys a checkout system they have not seen. Three real screens — cart, payment, sales report — will move signups more than any copy change on this list.

    Fix first
  • 03

    The testimonials section has no testimonials

    "What Our Users Say" sits above an empty space and a "Share Your Experience" button. An empty proof section reads worse than no section. Three quotes with a shop name and a photo, or remove it until you have them.

    Fix first
  • 04

    The free plan reads as a trap

    Promotion / Free / Usual US$20 mo — US$200 year raises three questions at once: free until when, and why is the yearly price ten months. Say plainly what stays free and for how long.

    This month
  • 05

    The hero says nothing concrete

    "Deploy your vision, expand your reach, and scale on demand" could sell any software. Try the sheet headline instead: Your whole shop. One screen. — then one line naming counter, website, payments, books.

    This month
  • 06

    Contact is on a different brand

    The footer sends people to hello@jicama.tech from kioscart.com. A merchant reads that as "this product might not last". Set up hello@kioscart.com and forward it.

    This month
  • 07

    No language switcher

    You are planning multi-lingual marketing but the site is English-only, with no hreflang tags. Ship two languages before the ads run, or the traffic converts at English-only rates.

    This month
  • 08

    No social links, in either direction

    The footer has no Instagram link and the site is not linked from a bio yet. Every ad rupee you spend leaks here.

    Quick
  • 09

    The footer still says 2025

    Two-minute fix, and dated copyright is the first thing a cautious buyer notices.

    Quick
  • 10

    The FAQ answers questions your AI could answer live

    You built an AI FAQ chat and then wrote a static accordion. Put the chat on the page and let it answer — it demonstrates the feature and answers the question in the same move.

    Quick

Rules for the translator

Numbers, currency symbols and the logo never translate. 14.58 stays 14.58.

No idioms were used, so nothing needs a cultural equivalent — translate literally and it will land.

For scripts that run longer (German, Tamil, Vietnamese), drop a word rather than reduce the type size. The hierarchy is doing the work, not the wording.

What you can change safely

The receipt items on Sheet 02 — swap Cold Brew and Croissant for whatever the local market actually sells. The numbers can change too; keep them to four characters.

The AI question on Sheet 03. Anything a shopkeeper would genuinely ask works better than a generic one.

Accent colours per market, if you want regional variants: the orange, teal and blue are separate tokens in the file.
