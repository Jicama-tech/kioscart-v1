KiosCart - Online Platform for Kiosk and Cart Management

KiosCart is an e-commerce platform that helps shopkeepers create and manage their online store. Manage products, process orders, accept payments, and grow your business — all from one dashboard.

What KiosCart Does

  • Online Storefront — Create a customizable store with your own URL to share with customers.
  • Product Management — Add products with variants, options, pricing, and inventory tracking.
  • Kiosk Mode — In-store POS system for walk-in customers.
  • Order Management — Track and manage customer orders in real-time.
  • Payment Tracking — Detect payment confirmations via email (Gmail integration) and match them to orders.
  • CRM — Manage your customer database, view purchase history, and communicate with customers.
  • Analytics — Sales reports, revenue tracking, and business insights.
  • WhatsApp Notifications — Automatic order and payment alerts via WhatsApp.
