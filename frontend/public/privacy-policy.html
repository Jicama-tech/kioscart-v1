Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

KiosCart ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Jicama.Tech. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform at https://kioscart.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number when you register.
  • Store Data: Product listings, orders, customer data that shopkeepers manage through our platform.
  • Payment Information: We do not store payment card details. Payment processing is handled by third-party providers (Razorpay, UPI).
  • Gmail Data (Optional): If a shopkeeper connects their Gmail, we read unread payment notification emails to match them with pending orders. We only store extracted metadata (amount, sender, reference ID) — not full email content.
  • Usage Data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, collected automatically.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our e-commerce platform services.
  • To process orders and facilitate communication between shopkeepers and customers.
  • To detect payment confirmations via email (Gmail integration, optional).
  • To send order notifications via WhatsApp.
  • To improve our services and user experience.

3. Gmail Data Usage

KiosCart's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only access Gmail data with explicit user consent, use it solely for matching payment emails to orders, and do not share it with third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Payment processors to complete transactions.
  • Cloud service providers for hosting and storage.
  • Law enforcement when required by law.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure authentication (JWT), and access controls to protect your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Revoke Gmail access at any time from Settings.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
